Put the real audio clips here as:

  happy-birthday-scream.mp3
  spindle-winding.mp3

Those exact filenames — js/content.js (CONFIG.box2.audioSrc and
CONFIG.box2.windAudioSrc) point to assets/audio/happy-birthday-scream.mp3
and assets/audio/spindle-winding.mp3. If you'd rather use different
filenames or formats (e.g. .m4a), just update those two lines in
content.js to match.

spindle-winding.mp3 plays on loop for the ~3s automatic wind-up on the
pop-up box screen and is cut off the instant the lid pops, so it doesn't
need to be a precise length — a few seconds of a ratchet/winding sound is
enough. Note: I can't download audio from the internet myself (no
browser/file-download tool here) — this file needs to come from you,
same as the video and images did.

Keep in mind the GitHub Pages site will be public by default, so this
audio clip (and everything else on the site) will be reachable by
anyone with the link, even though it won't be indexed/searchable unless
linked from elsewhere.
